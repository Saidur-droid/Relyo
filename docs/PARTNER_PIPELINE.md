# Relyo V2 — Partner Pipeline & Outreach System

> **STATUS: ACTIVE PARTNERSHIP PLAN**

## Objective

Use partnerships to distribute proof into ecosystems where software is already being created.

Relyo should approach partners as a **launch-success and trust layer**, not as a critic of their products.

## First-100 target allocation

Build the CRM as:

- 15 AI builders/platforms;
- 20 infrastructure/ecosystem partners;
- 30 agencies/studios;
- 20 accelerators/VC portfolio programs;
- 15 creators/educators.

Do not spray identical cold emails to 100 companies. Research each target and tailor the economic value.

## Partner qualification score

Score 1–5 on:

- overlap with initial stack;
- number of apps/users distributed;
- launch/reliability pain;
- technical integration fit;
- co-marketing fit;
- decision speed;
- strategic brand value.

Prioritize total score >= 24/35.

## Partner pitch by category

### Builder

> Your users create faster. Relyo helps them launch with independent proof across auth, payments, data, email and real customer journeys. We can start with a small API/MCP pilot and return evidence + Passport without taking over the builder relationship.

### Infrastructure provider

> We want to publish a verified launch checklist for your ecosystem and help users catch integration/configuration issues before support tickets or failed launches. Relyo complements your product and makes the provider look easier to use successfully.

### Agency

> Deliver clients a scoped Production Passport showing what was independently verified, what remains unknown, and when the proof expires. Use Relyo across your portfolio instead of manually repeating launch QA.

### Accelerator/VC

> We can run a Portfolio Proof Day before Demo Day or launch week and help founders verify launch-critical flows. You get fewer embarrassing demo failures and founders get concrete launch evidence.

### Creator

> Teach founders exactly what to verify before launch. Relyo gives your audience a practical free check instead of fear-based content.

## Partner economics hypotheses

### Agency

- portfolio discount;
- client-level billing;
- referral credit;
- optional expert certification later.

### Builder

- free sandbox;
- usage-based proof runs;
- minimum commit at scale;
- white-label premium.

### Creator

- only pay on verified paid activation;
- limited recurring share after economics validated.

### Accelerator/VC

- first Proof Day may be free/discounted for distribution;
- paid portfolio program later.

## Outreach cadence

Day 0: personalized email/DM.

Day 3: short follow-up with demo clip.

Day 8: send ecosystem-specific checklist/case study.

Day 15: close loop politely.

Do not spam.

## First partner win definition

A logo or meeting is not a win.

A win is one of:

- partner sends real apps into Relyo;
- partner initiates proof via API/MCP;
- partner publishes/co-markets a launch-proof resource;
- agency standardizes Relyo across multiple clients;
- accelerator runs a real portfolio proof program.

## Guardrails

- do not imply endorsement without permission;
- do not use partner logos without permission;
- do not publicly compare partner failure rates without rigorous methodology/consent;
- partner integrations must preserve verifier independence.
