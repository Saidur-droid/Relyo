# Relyo V2 — 100 Apps Verified Operating Playbook

> **STATUS: ACTIVE LAUNCH PROGRAM**

## Purpose

Create the first dense body of real production-learning, case studies, founder trust, and referral proof without attacking any builder platform.

Program name:

# **100 Apps Verified**

Promise:

> We help founders verify launch-critical production flows before their next launch, payment milestone, investor demo, or client handoff.

## Eligible apps

Prioritize:

- AI-built SaaS;
- indie SaaS;
- agency client apps;
- accelerator portfolio apps;
- apps using the initial Relyo stack;
- products close to launch or recently launched.

Include a mix of:

- Lovable;
- Replit;
- Cursor;
- Bolt;
- v0;
- Claude Code;
- Codex;
- traditional engineering.

Never publish builder “failure rankings.”

## Recruitment sources

1. Founder direct outreach.
2. Indie Hackers / startup communities.
3. LinkedIn/X founder posts.
4. AI-builder community groups.
5. Vercel/Supabase ecosystem communities.
6. Agencies/studios.
7. Accelerators/VC portfolios.
8. Creator partnerships.
9. Referrals from participating founders.
10. Product Hunt pre-launch community.

## Recruitment message

> We are selecting 100 early SaaS products for Relyo’s launch-verification program. You keep building with the tools you prefer. We help check critical production flows such as auth, data access, payments, email and real customer journeys, and give you a scoped evidence-backed launch report/Passport. Early participants get founder-level support in exchange for product feedback.

## Intake form

Collect only what is necessary:

- founder/team contact;
- public URL;
- repo/provider connection willingness;
- build stack;
- launch moment;
- auth/payment/email providers;
- critical customer journey;
- permission for anonymized aggregate learning;
- separate opt-in for public case study.

## Consent levels

### Level A — private proof only

No public mention.

### Level B — anonymized aggregate data

May contribute to aggregate reliability research.

### Level C — public case study

Founder approves final wording/screenshots before publication.

Never bundle public case-study consent into basic product terms.

## Onboarding flow

```text
intake
→ connect URL/repo/providers
→ identify launch moment
→ map Production Graph
→ select proof contracts
→ execute checks
→ classify unknowns/failures
→ support safe fixes
→ re-verify
→ issue Passport
→ collect feedback
```

## Founder feedback interview

Ask:

1. What did you think was ready before Relyo?
2. What surprised you?
3. Which finding mattered most?
4. Was anything confusing or noisy?
5. Would you pay for this before launch?
6. What price would feel reasonable?
7. Would you keep proof continuously active?
8. Would you show the Passport to a customer/investor/client?
9. What would make you recommend Relyo?

## Case-study structure

Do not shame the builder.

Use:

```text
What they were launching
→ what they wanted confidence in
→ what Relyo checked
→ what needed attention
→ what was fixed
→ what Relyo independently re-verified
→ business outcome
```

Headline example:

> How a founder verified signup, payment and cancellation before accepting the first paying users.

## Referral loop

After successful proof:

> Know another founder launching soon? Give them one free launch check.

Possible incentive:

- proof credit;
- one free re-check;
- subscription credit.

Avoid cash rewards until fraud controls exist.

## Program milestones

### Apps 1–10

Goal: product learning.

Founder participates manually in every run.

### Apps 11–25

Goal: repeatable onboarding + first proof contracts.

### Apps 26–50

Goal: identify recurring failure classes and pricing signal.

### Apps 51–75

Goal: referral loop + agency/accelerator distribution.

### Apps 76–100

Goal: publish first rigorous aggregate learnings and convert best users to recurring plans.

## Metrics

- applications submitted;
- acceptance rate;
- onboarding completion;
- time to first meaningful finding;
- proof completion;
- critical unknown/failure frequency;
- remediation success;
- founder NPS/recommendation intent;
- willingness to pay;
- paid conversion;
- Passport sharing;
- referrals generated;
- support minutes/app.

## Success criteria

The program succeeds if it produces:

- 100 real apps with documented proof runs;
- high-quality failure/outcome data;
- 10+ strong case studies;
- repeatable paid conversion signal;
- agency/accelerator relationships;
- first trustworthy Relyo reliability report;
- evidence that users return for proof after subsequent releases.
