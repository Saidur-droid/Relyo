# Relyo — Investor One-Pager

> **AI builds it. Relyo proves it.**

## Company

Relyo is the **independent proof layer for machine-built software**.

AI builders and coding agents are making software creation dramatically faster. The downstream trust problem remains: authentication, authorization, payments, data isolation, configuration, email, recovery, cost, and real customer journeys still need to work in production.

Relyo connects to the app, maps the production system, executes versioned Proof Contracts, safely repairs selected failures, independently re-verifies outcomes, and issues a signed Production Passport for the exact release/environment.

Software creation is shifting from human-written to machine-produced at large scale.

Current market signals:

- GitHub reported 180M+ developers and massive AI-assisted development adoption.
- Vercel reported agent-initiated deployments becoming a material and rapidly growing share of deployment volume.
- AI builder companies such as Lovable and Replit have demonstrated extraordinary user/revenue growth and large financing rounds.
- Developer surveys and software delivery research continue to show that AI speed does not remove accuracy, security, stability, or operational risk.
