# Relyo V2 — 30/60/90-Day Launch Calendar

> **STATUS: ACTIVE EXECUTION CALENDAR**
>
> Baseline start date: **2026-09-15**. Update dates when the actual build start changes.

## North-star for first 90 days

Move Relyo from strategy to a visible product with real user learning:

```text
URL/repo check
→ app-specific risk discovery
→ Verify My Launch
→ first Passport
→ first paid proofs
→ 100 Apps Verified
→ first partner conversations
```

## Roles

Initial owner assumptions:

- Founder/CEO: product narrative, customer discovery, founder-led demos, partnerships, fundraising prep.
- Product/Infra Engineer 1: Trust Kernel, API, data model.
- Product/Infra Engineer 2: web app, URL check, onboarding, Passport UI.
- Reliability/Security Engineer: runner, proof contracts, evidence, threat model.
- Design/Product Generalist: landing pages, onboarding, Passport UX, launch assets.

If team is smaller, founder temporarily owns customer, marketing, partnerships, and manual proof operations.

# Weekly operating cadence

Every Monday:

- review funnel metrics;
- pick highest-leverage contract/growth blocker;
- choose 1 product improvement, 1 distribution improvement, 1 trust improvement.

Every Friday:

- demo product live;
- publish one useful lesson;
- update Verified Failure Graph learnings;
- update top 10 customer objections.

## Stop conditions

Reprioritize if:

- free scans do not produce meaningful findings;
- users do not understand the Passport;
- paid conversion is below hypothesis after targeted fixes;
- proof cost makes Launch Proof economically impossible;
- false positives/false verified risk rises;
- partner conversations show stronger pull for a different launch moment.
