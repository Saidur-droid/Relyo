# Relyo V2 — Company Operating Plan

> **STATUS: ACTIVE COMPANY OPERATING PLAN**

## Operating principle

Keep the company lean until repeatable activation and paid conversion are visible.

## First team shape

### Founder / CEO

Owns:

- product direction;
- customer discovery;
- category narrative;
- partnerships;
- fundraising;
- first sales.

### Product/Infra Engineer 1

Owns:

- Trust Kernel;
- API/control plane;
- data model;
- provider adapters.

### Product/Infra Engineer 2

Owns:

- web app;
- onboarding;
- URL/repo scan UX;
- Passport UI;
- billing.

### Reliability/Security Engineer

Owns:

- runner;
- contract execution;
- browser journeys;
- threat model;
- evidence integrity;
- credential architecture.

### Design/Product Generalist

Owns:

- launch-moment UX;
- landing pages;
- product copy;
- design system;
- case-study assets.

### Growth/DevRel Engineer — hire after activation signal

Owns:

- docs/CLI/GitHub Action;
- technical content;
- community;
- partner integrations;
- launch events.

## Hiring order

1. strong technical co-builder/lead engineer if missing;
2. second product/infra engineer;
3. security/reliability engineer;
4. design/product generalist;
5. growth/devrel engineer;
6. sales/partnership hire only after repeatable partner motion.

## Compensation/equity principles

- preserve enough option pool for high-impact early hires;
- use market-appropriate cash adjusted for geography/runway;
- meaningful equity for truly early risk-taking employees;
- four-year vesting with one-year cliff is common but legal counsel should confirm local/Delaware structure;
- contractor IP assignment required.

Do not hardcode equity percentages before legal/cap-table design.

## Incorporation/IP

Before institutional financing:

- determine Delaware C-Corp or other investor-compatible structure with counsel;
- ensure founder IP assignment;
- ensure contractor/employee invention assignment;
- confirm trademark/domain strategy for Relyo;
- set privacy policy/Terms before handling production integrations at scale;
- establish data processing terms for enterprise customers later.

## Burn philosophy

First 6 months:

> spend on product quality, proof execution, security, and distribution loops — not prestige.

Avoid:

- expensive office;
- celebrity CMO;
- large sales team;
- broad paid advertising;
- unnecessary conferences;
- custom enterprise work without strategic value.

## Runway targets

Before pre-seed:

- keep burn minimal and milestone-driven.

After pre-seed:

- target roughly 18–24 months runway where practical;
- review monthly burn and proof-cost exposure;
- hiring plan must map to milestone outcomes.

## Security/compliance milestones

### Before private alpha

- threat model;
- secrets policy;
- access controls;
- logging/redaction;
- incident response basics;
- dependency scanning;
- backups for Relyo-owned critical data.

### Before public beta

- documented subprocessors;
- privacy policy/ToS;
- vulnerability disclosure channel;
- audit logging;
- access reviews;
- production monitoring;
- credential rotation policy.

### Before serious enterprise selling

Evaluate based on demand:

- SOC 2 Type I/II;
- SSO/SCIM;
- data residency;
- private runners;
- customer-managed keys;
- security questionnaire readiness;
- penetration testing.

Do not claim certifications before achieved.

## Weekly company cadence

Monday:

- product/growth KPI review;
- choose top three company priorities.

Tuesday–Thursday:

- build + customer sessions.

Friday:

- live demo;
- growth review;
- security/reliability review;
- update repo continuity.

Monthly:

- financial runway;
- hiring;
- pricing economics;
- strategy assumptions versus real evidence;
- partner pipeline;
- fundraising readiness.

## Decision framework

Prioritize work that improves at least one:

- trust correctness;
- activation speed;
- paid conversion;
- shareability;
- platform distribution;
- failure intelligence;
- proof economics.

Deprioritize work that only makes the product look larger.
