# Relyo Market Evidence Library

> **Purpose:** give future product, growth, fundraising, and AI agents a source-controlled evidence base for public claims. Do not invent statistics. Always re-verify time-sensitive numbers before external publication.

---

## AI developer adoption and trust

### Stack Overflow 2025 Developer Survey

Useful claims:

- 84% of respondents use or plan to use AI tools in development.
- 51% of professional developers use AI tools daily.
- 66% cited AI solutions that are “almost right, but not quite” as a frustration.
- 45% said debugging AI-generated code is more time-consuming.
- 46% distrust AI-tool accuracy versus 33% who trust it.
- 76% in the workflow question said they do not plan to use AI for deployment and monitoring.

Source:
https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai

Use for:

- “AI speed does not remove verification needs.”
- founder education;
- investor why-now slides.

Do not use for:

- claiming every AI-generated app is broken.

## Marketing claim rules

Before public use:

1. verify the source is still live/current;
2. verify the date;
3. quote/paraphrase carefully;
4. state sample limitations;
5. distinguish survey results from population facts;
6. distinguish benchmark code-generation results from production-app prevalence;
7. distinguish Relyo customer data from industry data;
8. never claim all AI/vibe-coded apps fail;
9. never imply a partner's product is unsafe because of a general industry statistic;
10. always turn fear/risk into a practical checklist and verification path.
