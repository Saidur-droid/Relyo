# Outreach priority waves

## Wave 1 — immediately after alpha works

- 10 founders/design partners;
- 5 agencies;
- 3 accelerator/VC portfolio operators;
- 3 small builder/platform teams;
- 5 infra ecosystem/community contacts.

## Wave 2 — after first 25 verified apps

- bigger agency group;
- co-marketing with provider ecosystems;
- creators;
- PearX/Antler/YC applications if timing fits.

## Wave 3 — after first 100 verified apps

- larger builders;
- institutional pre-seed/seed investors;
- enterprise design partners.

## Success metric

Do not measure meetings. Measure:

- apps referred;
- proof runs initiated;
- partner-originated paid accounts;
- embedded API/MCP usage;
- portfolio/agency standardization.
